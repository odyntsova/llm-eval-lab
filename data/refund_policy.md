# GadgetHub Refund Policy

1. **Refund window:** Items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
2. **Condition:** Items must be unused and in original packaging.
   Opened electronics can be returned, but a 15% restocking fee applies.
3. **Non-refundable items:** Gift cards, digital downloads, and personalized items.
4. **Defective items:** Defective items can be returned within 90 days of delivery
   for a full refund or replacement. No restocking fee. GadgetHub pays return shipping.
5. **Return shipping:** The customer pays a $7.99 return shipping fee, unless the item is defective.
6. **Refund method and timing:** Refunds go to the original payment method within
   5–7 business days after the item arrives at our warehouse.
7. **How to start a return:** Go to Account → Orders → Request Return.
8. **Approval:** All returns and refunds are confirmed by a human agent before the money is sent.
